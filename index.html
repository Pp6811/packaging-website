About Us

A Factory Built on Craft & Conscience

Founded over a decade ago, EcoPack Studio has grown from a small workshop into a full-scale packaging manufacturer serving clients across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

We believe packaging is the first physical touchpoint between a brand and its customer. That's why every box we produce is designed to create a moment of delight — sustainably.

Our 8,000 sqm facility houses state-of-the-art printing, die-cutting, and finishing equipment, operated by a team of 120+ skilled craftspeople committed to quality in every detail.
